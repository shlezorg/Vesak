Happy Vesak Day
සුභ වෙසක් මංගල්‍යයක් වේවා!

May the radiant light of Vesak illuminate your heart with boundless peace, ancient wisdom, and loving-kindness.

On this sacred Poya night, as a thousand lanterns rise toward the heavens, may your spirit be lifted with compassion, your mind be stilled in mindfulness, and your life be blessed with dhamma.
